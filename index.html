The Cardiovascular Crisis in India

The Problem

Cardiovascular diseases cause over 25% of deaths in India, with early diagnosis severely lacking in rural and primary care settings.

  • Traditional stethoscopes depend heavily on clinician expertise, leading to subjective and inconsistent results
  • Rural clinics lack affordable, smart diagnostic tools, causing delayed detection and treatment
  • Patients face late diagnosis while health workers need reliable, easy-to-use devices
  • Limited access to specialized cardiac care in remote areas

Our Solution

AUSCSYNC is an AI-powered stethoscope that analyzes heart sounds in real-time for early cardiac diagnosis.

  • Combines advanced sensors, edge AI, and mobile integration for portability and accessibility
  • Multilingual user interface designed specifically for rural healthcare workers
  • Affordable pricing model to ensure widespread adoption
  • Real-time diagnostic feedback to support clinical decision making
